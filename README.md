## 📖 What Is The Twilight Forest?

**The Twilight Forest** is an adventure dimension mod for Minecraft. You craft a portal with water and flowers, throw a diamond in, and step into a world where the sun never fully sets. The dimension is structured as a **progression chain** — each boss drops an item needed to unlock the next area.

It's a classic "boss rush" style mod with hand-crafted dungeons, custom mobs, unique loot, and a sense of discovery that few modern mods capture.

**Key highlights:**
- ✅ **Entire dimension** — dense forest, fire swamp, dark forest, highlands, and more
- ✅ **Boss progression** — Naga → Lich → Minoshroom → Hydra → Knight Phantom → Ur-Ghast → Alpha Yeti → Snow Queen → Final Boss
- ✅ **Hand-built dungeons** — Hollow Hill, Labyrinth, Dark Tower, Yeti Cave, Auroral Castle
- ✅ **Unique loot** — Fiery armor, Steeleaf, Knightmetal, Ice gear
- ✅ **Huge trees** — Canopy trees taller than anything in vanilla
- ✅ **Magic map** — Tracks progress across the dimension
